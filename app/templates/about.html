How signing in works

There are no passwords. Enter your email and we send a 6-digit code that's valid for 10 minutes — enter it and you're signed in.

We never store your email address itself. Instead we keep a one-way SHA-256 hash of it (the same scheme Gravatar uses), so if our database were ever breached, it wouldn't reveal which entries belong to which email address.

That hash also doubles as your Gravatar id — if you have an avatar set up at gravatar.com, it shows up automatically once you're signed in.

Each account's weigh-ins are private and only visible to that account.
